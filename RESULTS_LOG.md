### What was done
- Data: the cached Swiss pool `data/_combined_pool_cache.pkl` (HEAPO + Kaiser et al., calendar 2023, 15 min) and the WPuQ pool `data/_wpuq_pool_cache.pkl`. Kloten: 50 submetered HPs, 1307 households without an HP or other electric heating.

### Implications
- For the paper: the code base on this branch is the one behind the draft. No claim changes.
- For the next steps:
  1. **The capacity proxy is the 99.9th percentile of each HP's 15-min draw** (`hp_capacity.robust_series_peak`), not the plain maximum the text describes (II-B, after eq. 4). The plain maximum is 4% higher per HP (median; D3 in `REVISION_LOG.md`). Step 2's "capacities of the added HPs" and P̄_unit will use the same robust peak, so c and P̄_unit match the ground truth of Table IV. Step 11b covers the alternatives.
  2. **The published design draws all splits from one generator** (`default_rng(0)` consumed in order), so seeds are not fixed per split. Step 0 keeps this, as it must. New steps will seed each split from `SeedSequence(seed).spawn(20)`, so one split can be rerun on its own.
  3. **A split-pool non-TCL correction already exists**: `outputs.kloten_nontcl_correction` (60 splits, halves the non-HP pool, σ at each target's own T_h, correction N·σ). It is not in the paper. Step 3 will follow the prompt's design (20 splits, fixed point on N̂_hp) and report this earlier variant next to it, not instead of it.
  4. **Raw HEAPO 15-min files are not on disk.** `data/heapo_data/` holds only `meta_data/` and `reports/`; the Kaiser et al. raw files (2447) are present. Step 0 ran from the cached pools only. This matters for Step 1.1 (other winters), since the HEAPO side of Kloten cannot be re-read for other years without re-downloading HEAPO (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.15056919).
- Decisions for Alex: none at this step.

### Confidence and caveats
- High confidence for the Kloten numbers: the rebuilt design is bit-identical to the paper cache.
- The reproduction runs from cached pools, not from the raw files. A rebuild from raw HEAPO data was not tested, since the raw 15-min HEAPO files are absent.
- The 15-min SF numbers were not generated by any function in the repository before this step; `planning._sf15_kloten` recomputes them (sum of the 50 HP loads over the sum of their robust peaks) and matches the draft.
