## Step 1 — Inventory of the data

### What was done
- Kloten members as in Step 0: 50 HP households (26 HEAPO, 24 Kaiser et al. "Swiss"), 1307 households without an HP (1306 Swiss, 1 HEAPO).
- 1.1: share of 15-min intervals present per window. Swiss: every raw file of the members (HP households on both the dwelling and the paired HP meter, the minimum of the two). HEAPO: `data/_heapo_daily_coverage.parquet`, the daily interval counts kept from the earlier download, since the raw HEAPO files are not on disk. Windows: Nov–Mar winters 2019/20 to 2024/25, and the heating months (Jan–Mar, Nov–Dec) of calendar 2023 and 2024. Threshold ≥ 90%.
- 1.2: HEAPO `Installation_HasPVSystem`; Swiss `0_max_net_injection` and the exported energy `kWh_to_grid` in the raw files. The dataset paper (Kaiser et al., `data/Swiss_dataset/2607.19294v1.pdf`, Sec. 2.1–2.3) was read for the PV and control rules.
- 1.3: per HP, near zero = below 5% of its robust peak. Cold days = daily mean below 0 °C (19 days, 13 of them weekdays). Share of HPs near zero, and the SF of the 50 HPs, against local time; per HP, windows of ≥ 30 min in which it is near zero on ≥ 80% of the cold weekdays.
- 1.4: HEAPO protocols (`reports/protocols.csv`); the Swiss dataset has none.
- 1.5: household structure of the Hg, MqO and Hamelin pools.
- Code: `planning.step1_inventory()` (with `inventory_years`, `inventory_pv`, `blocking_profile`, `blocked_windows`, `fig_blocking`, `inventory_nameplate`, `inventory_real`); notebook section "Step 1". Pickles `results/planning/step1_*.pkl`.
- Deviations: none.

- KLO temperatures (MeteoSwiss, daily mean): 2023 coldest −8.1 °C, 18 days below 0 °C; **2024 coldest −4.7 °C**, 19 days below 0 °C. The span from T_h ≈ 16.6 °C to the coldest day shrinks from about 25 °C to about 21 °C, a useful contrast for the planning estimate (iv).
- Pilot tariffs: 11 of the 24 Swiss HP households moved to a pilot grid tariff on 1 Oct 2023 (4 ToU, 4 dynamic, 3 flat); 2 of them had their HP under automatic pilot control. 330 of the 1306 Swiss non-HP households are on pilot tariffs. Per the dataset paper (Table 5), ripple control applied to 14 Sep 2023, no control from 20 Dec 2023 to Jan/Feb 2024, and pilot control on 18 Jul–4 Sep and 19 Nov–19 Dec 2024. **1.2 PV.**

| | HP households | Non-HP households |
|---|---|---|

- Neither pool is net of PV. Both keep only imported energy: HEAPO `kWh_received_Other` (non-HP circuit) and Swiss `kWh_to_installation`. A PV household therefore enters the aggregates with its grid import, its export discarded. Real feeder net load would subtract the export.
- HP load is the HP submeter in both datasets, so the capacity ground truth is unaffected.
- The Swiss dataset removes the whole year of a new PV installation for privacy, so these 16 Swiss PV households had PV throughout their data.
- Open: whether HEAPO `kWh_received_Other` is metered behind the PV system (import after self-consumption) or as gross consumption. Not resolvable without the HEAPO documentation or raw files (*needs Alex*).

**1.3 Blocking periods: clearly visible.** Figure: `figures/planning/fig_step1_blocking.pdf`.
- Cold weekdays: the share of HPs near zero rises from about 0.33 to **0.77–0.80 at 11:00–12:00**, and from about 0.35 to **0.57–0.66 at 17:30–18:45**. Cold weekends show neither step. The 11:00–12:00 block matches the example HP of the dataset paper (Fig. 1d) and its statement that HPs "can be blocked for up to four hours a day".
- **35 of 50 HPs** show a recurring block: 34 at 11:00–12:00 (18 HEAPO, 16 Swiss) and 20 at about 18:00–18:30. 11 show none.
- SF of the 50 HPs on cold weekdays: **0.33 before the midday block, 0.10 during it, and a rebound to 0.46 at 12:15–12:30**. In the evening it falls to 0.15 and rebounds to 0.45 at 19:15. Weekends stay at 0.30–0.38 over the same hours.
- Annual peaks: the 50-HP load peaks at 12:30 on 23 Jan 2023, a rebound; 11 of its 36 top-0.1% intervals fall in the 45 min after a weekday release. The whole-house load of the 50 HP households peaks at 19:15 on 4 Dec 2023, the evening rebound. The 1307 non-HP households peak at 17:45–18:45, inside the evening block.
- The draft's 0.56 peak SF is on 3 Dec 2023, a Sunday, so it is not a rebound value.

### Implications
- For the paper:
  - The draft says the net load is what a DSO meters at the feeder (II, eq. 1). In the Kloten data it is a sum of grid imports, export discarded, and half the HP households have PV. This is not wrong for the estimate, but reviewers may ask. Step 10b sizes it.
  - The Limitations paragraph says blocking "may bias the net-load sensitivity and the capacity estimate downwards" and "is not a visible source of error" on the real aggregates. It is visible in the Kloten load itself: 35 of 50 HPs are blocked daily on weekdays. The capacity estimate already includes this, since pilot and target are both blocked. The sentence can now cite a measured block.
- For the next steps:
  - **Steps 2 and 4 (peak metric):** the annual 15-min maximum of HP-rich aggregates is set by the rebound after blocking, at 12:15–12:30 or about 19:15, not by free-running HPs. The ground truth includes this automatically. But c from Step 2 will be specific to this ripple-control regime: the evening block keeps HPs off the non-HP peak at 17:45–18:45, which lowers c. I will report c separately for weekdays and weekends as a diagnostic, and add the 99.9th percentile metric, which is less sensitive to one rebound interval.
  - **Step 4 (draws):** 330 of the 1306 Swiss non-HP households and 11 of 24 Swiss HP households were on pilot tariffs in Oct–Dec 2023, which includes the coldest days. This is already true of the published results; I will add it to the caveats and not filter, unless Alex prefers otherwise.
  - **Step 6:** removing a whole HP household (100% penetration) is not the operation Step 2's c describes, which replaces a non-HP household with an HP household. The prediction needs a removal increment from the same pilot side.
- Decisions for Alex:
  1. **Step 7 design.** (a) Recommended: pilot = the 26 HEAPO HPs fitted on 2023, targets = aggregates of the 22 Swiss HPs and Swiss non-HP households, scored in 2023 and in 2024. There is no leakage, the same target households appear in both years, and 2024 is 3.4 °C milder on its coldest day. The cost: the pilot is from another source (and almost all PV), and the result has no spread across pilot splits. (b) Swiss-only 11/11 pilot/target splits over 20 splits: same source, but very small halves. (c) Re-download HEAPO (458 MB) to add Nov–Dec 2022 for 22 HEAPO HPs: a partial winter. I recommend (a), with (b) as a robustness row and the pilot-tariff households flagged.
  2. **Step 6 increment.** Recommended: calibrate a removal increment on the Kloten pilot side (remove whole HP households from 100%-penetration pilot aggregates), next to Step 2's replacement c.
  3. **HEAPO `kWh_received_Other` and PV** (1.2): do you know from the HEAPO documentation whether it is behind the PV system? It decides how Step 10b treats the 25 HEAPO PV households.
  4. **Pilot-tariff households** (Steps 2–5): keep as in the paper (recommended, for comparability with Table IV), or exclude as a sensitivity?

### Confidence and caveats
- HEAPO coverage relies on the kept daily counts, not on the raw files. Counts of 0 or 96 per day are taken at face value.
- "Near zero" uses 5% of the robust peak. An HP can idle on its thermostat at any time, so the block is identified by its clock regularity across cold weekdays, not by single zeros. 13 cold weekdays is a small sample, but the 11:00–12:00 step (0.33 → 0.78) is far outside day-to-day variation.
- Swiss PV detection follows the dataset's own rule (export > 0.025 kWh in ≥ 10 intervals). Households that self-consume nearly everything would not be flagged.
